The Craft

Where tradition
meets tomorrow

Every Vayomeie piece is born in the heart of Andhra Pradesh, where artisans have been weaving stories into fabric for generations. We work directly with local craftswomen, ensuring every stitch carries both cultural legacy and fair livelihood.

We use natural dyes derived from indigo, pomegranate, madder and myrobalan — ancient ingredients that colour with integrity.
